03 / HOW I APPROACH THINGS

A process, not a formula.

The steps shift from project to project. These are the habits I try to return to.

  1. 01

    Notice

    Start with a question, a need or something that doesn’t quite make sense.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Read, observe, ask people, collect evidence and challenge first assumptions.

  3. 03

    Make

    Try an approach, build a draft or test an idea in the real world.

  4. 04

    Reflect

    Look honestly at the outcome, document what changed and decide what comes next.
